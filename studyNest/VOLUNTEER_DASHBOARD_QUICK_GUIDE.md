3. Click the badge to open the modal
4. View your analytics

## 🎯 Key Metrics

### What Each Metric Means

| Metric | Description | How It Increases |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Total Responses** | Number of hall requests you've answered | Answer more requests |
| **Feedback Received** | Number of feedback comments from students | Students rate your responses |
| **Avg Rating** | Average star rating (1-5) | Get higher star ratings |
| **Total Points** | Cumulative points from responses | Each response = ~5 points |
| **Current Level** | Your volunteer tier (1-5) | Accumulate points |
| **% to Next Level** | Progress toward advancement | Answer more requests |

## 🏆 Achievement Badges

Badges are automatically awarded:

```
🏆 Top Contributor
   └─ Unlock at: 50+ responses
   └─ Benefit: Recognition for active participation

⭐ Highly Rated
   └─ Unlock at: 4.5+ average rating + 5+ reviews
   └─ Benefit: Shows quality of responses

🎖️ Expert Volunteer
   └─ Unlock at: Level 4 or higher
   └─ Benefit: Advanced tier recognition

❤️ Community Favorite
   └─ Unlock at: 100+ feedback received
   └─ Benefit: Very high engagement recognition
```

## 🔄 How Points Work

```
1. You answer a request
   ↓
2. Student rates your response (1-5 stars)
   ↓
3. You earn:
   • 5 points for submitting response
   • Bonus points based on rating quality
   ↓
4. Points accumulate → Level up!
```

**Typical Progression:**
- To reach Level 2: ~50 points (~10 responses)
- To reach Level 3: ~100 points (~20 responses)
- To reach Level 4: ~200 points (~40 responses)
- To reach Level 5: ~350 points (~70 responses)

---

## 🎓 Tips for Leveling Up

1. **Increase Responses**
   - Answer more hall requests
   - Each response = ~5 points
   - More volume = faster leveling

2. **Improve Rating**
   - Provide accurate information
   - Be helpful and detailed
   - Higher ratings = progress faster

3. **Consistency**
   - Regular participation
   - Build feedback history
   - Aim for 100+ reviews for badges

4. **Quality Over Quantity**
   - Thoughtful responses score higher
   - Accurate info gets better ratings
   - Long-term reputation matters

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How often does the dashboard update?**
A: In real-time! Data is fetched fresh each time you open it.

**Q: Can I undo feedback I received?**
A: No, but you can improve future responses to get better ratings.

**Q: What happens at Level 5?**
A: You're a Master Volunteer! You've reached the maximum level. Keep responding and earning recognition!

**Q: How do I get specific badges?**
A: See the Achievement Badges section above for exact requirements.

**Q: Where can I see all my feedback?**
A: The modal shows the last 3 comments. Complete feedback history is part of your profile.

**Q: Can I lose points or levels?**
A: No - points and levels only go up, never down.
